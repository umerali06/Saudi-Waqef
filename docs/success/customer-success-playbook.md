# Customer Success Playbook

## Goals
- Reduce time-to-value for new companies.
- Increase feature adoption in accounting and HR.
- Reduce recurring support issues by proactive education.

## Onboarding flow
1) Welcome email with setup steps and video guide.
2) Complete company setup and chart of accounts.
3) Import customers, vendors, and opening balances.
4) Configure VAT, payroll, and approval settings.
5) First invoice + first payroll run.

## Health score signals
- Weekly active usage.
- Invoices created in last 14 days.
- Payroll runs created in last 30 days.
- Support tickets per user.
- Successful VAT filing runs.

## Touchpoints
- Day 7: usage check-in.
- Day 30: feature adoption review.
- Quarterly: business review and optimization.

## Retention strategies
- Proactive education via knowledge base.
- Monthly product update webinar.
- Early warning outreach for low health scores.
