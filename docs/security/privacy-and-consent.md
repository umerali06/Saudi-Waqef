# Privacy and Consent Policy (Draft)

## Purpose
This policy explains how Saudi Waqef collects, uses, and protects customer data.

## Data Categories
- Account data: name, email, role.
- Company data: legal name, VAT settings, billing details.
- HR/PII: national ID, iqama, passport number, employee contact data.
- Finance data: invoices, bills, journal entries.

## Lawful Basis
- Contractual necessity to provide accounting and HR services.
- Legitimate interests in security monitoring and fraud prevention.

## Consent
- Users must acknowledge the privacy policy during onboarding.
- Admins can manage notification preferences for system communications.

## Data Retention
- Operational data retained per `docs/ops/data-retention.md`.
- Support tickets retained for audit and compliance requirements.

## Data Subject Requests
- Admins can request export or deletion through the support process.
- Requests must be logged and completed within 30 days.

## Security Controls
- Encryption at rest for PII.
- MFA for admin roles.
- Audit logging for sensitive access and changes.
