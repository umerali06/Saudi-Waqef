# Business Continuity Plan

## Manual operations
- Capture invoices/expenses in offline template.
- Log payroll adjustments manually for approval.
- Use CSV template to batch import once restored.

## Critical data exports
- Daily export of GL summary.
- Weekly export of payroll register.
- VAT period summary export pre-filing.

## Review cadence
- Review and update after major releases.
- Conduct tabletop exercise every 6 months.
