# KPI Monitoring Plan

## Daily for first 30 days
- Active companies
- Invoices per company
- Payroll runs
- Support ticket volume
- MRR and churn

## Weekly review
- Onboarding conversion rate
- Support volume trends
- Billing failures or churn spikes

## Alerts
- Onboarding completion rate < 60%
- Support tickets > 20% week-over-week increase
- MRR drop > 10% week-over-week
