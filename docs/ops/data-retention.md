# Data Retention & Privacy

## Retention Policy
- Audit logs: 2 years.
- Financial transactions: 7 years.
- HR records: 5 years after employee exit.
- Login attempt logs: 90 days.
- Backups: 30 daily + 12 monthly.

## Deletion Requests
- Verify requester identity.
- Export data before deletion if legally required.
- Remove personal data from Firestore and Cloudinary.
