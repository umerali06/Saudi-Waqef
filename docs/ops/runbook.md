# Ops Runbook

## Incident Response
1. Acknowledge alert and open incident ticket.
2. Identify scope (tenant vs system-wide).
3. Mitigate (rollback, feature flag, or rate limiting).
4. Communicate status to stakeholders.
5. Post-incident review within 48 hours.

## Maintenance Window
- Schedule after business hours (KSA time).
- Notify customers 48 hours in advance.
- Take a pre-maintenance backup.

## Upgrade Policy
- Monthly dependency review.
- Quarterly security audit and penetration test.
