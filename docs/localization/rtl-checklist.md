# RTL Checklist

## Layout
- Inputs and selects align to the right in RTL.
- Tables and cards mirror correctly.
- Icons and arrows align with direction.

## Content
- Arabic labels fit without truncation.
- Helper text and errors align to RTL.

## Formatting
- Dates in `ar-SA` style.
- SAR currency formatting.
- Arabic-Indic numerals in Arabic locale.

## Browsers
- Chrome, Edge, and Safari coverage.
